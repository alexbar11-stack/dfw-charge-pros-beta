Answer objections early

Questions homeowners ask before booking.

Will my electrical panel support an EV charger?

A qualified electrician needs to review the panel, available capacity, breaker space, charger requirements and intended cable route. This concept asks for those details early instead of assuming every home is ready.

Should I buy the charger before requesting an estimate?

The page should explain which charger types the installer supports after the company confirms its capabilities. Until then, customers can indicate whether they already own equipment or need guidance.

What determines installation cost?

Common scope drivers can include electrical capacity, circuit distance, wall or trench routing, charger requirements and permit needs. The official company should confirm its estimating and permitting policies before publishing specifics.

Does Hardwire Electric repair existing EV chargers?

Yes. The current official page states that the company offers EV-charger repair and maintenance in addition to installation.
